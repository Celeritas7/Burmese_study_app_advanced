## 1. English adjective order: Opinion before Fact

**Sources:** 1X-omQGs5qDaQmUkQW4mJgGW6ih0KoAs2, 1MMVmy-Czshc_mDL349LNPcMg5itVxso6, 1rUrUT_hFmtMJ7wck_2NYer9imLITRAGC, 1kuuqCwMl4vXVvt757XzHc28Y7PJ1UhxG

**Pattern:** (article) + **Opinion adjective** + **Fact adjective** + **Noun**

Rule slide, exactly as shown:

| Burmese (on slide) | Romanisation (inferred) | English |
|---|---|---|
| Opinion နဲ့ Fact ယှဉ်လာရင် | Opinion nè Fact shin la yin | When an Opinion and a Fact (adjective) come together… |
| Opinion ကို အရင်ရေးပါ။ | Opinion ko a-yin yay ba. | …write the Opinion first. |
| Fact ကို noun နား ကပ်ရေးပါ။ | Fact ko noun na kat yay ba. | Write the Fact right next to the noun. |

Explanation: an opinion adjective says what the speaker thinks (beautiful, nice, horrible). A fact adjective describes something objective (size, age, colour, nationality). The opinion adjective comes first, and the fact adjective goes closest to the noun.

Quiz slide (1kuuqCwMl4vXVvt757XzHc28Y7PJ1UhxG): "tall ?" / "beautiful ?" asks which of the two is opinion and which is fact.

### Burmese grammar these slides show (inferred commentary, not taught on screen)

- **Adjective as a relative clause, V + တဲ့ + N** (colloquial): လှတဲ့ ကား "a car that is pretty", ချောတဲ့သူ "a person who is handsome", ဆိုးလိုက်တဲ့ ခွေး "what a nasty dog".
- **V + သော** is the formal/literary equivalent of တဲ့. It appears in every dictionary-style gloss: ရှည်သော, တိုသော, ငယ်ရွယ်သော, အသက်ကြီးသော, ဆိုးရွားသော, ချစ်စရာကောင်းသော.
- **V1 + ပြီး + V2 တဲ့ N** joins two adjectives: ငယ်ရွယ်ပြီး ချောတဲ့သူ "a person who is young and handsome".
- **V + လိုက်တဲ့** is emphatic/exclamatory: ဆိုးလိုက်တဲ့ "so horrible / what a horrible…".
- **Colour after the noun:** ကားအနက် "car-black" = a black car. Compare လှတဲ့ ကား, where the descriptive clause comes before the noun.
- **ကြီး as an augmentative suffix:** ခွေးကြီး "big dog", လူဆန်းကြီး "strange (old/big) guy".
- **တစ်ယောက်** is the classifier for people: ချောတဲ့သူ တစ်ယောက် "one handsome person".
