Grammar points that show up in these Burmese lines (inferred, as teaching notes for the app):
- **ရဲ့** is the possessive "'s / of".
- **ပဲ … သေးတယ်** means "only … still / yet".
- **မ + V + ပါနဲ့** is a polite negative command ("don't …"), e.g. လျှော့မတွက်ပါနဲ့.
- **V + ပါသေးတယ်** means "still …".
- **တောင်** means "even": နားလည်သူတွေတောင်, "even those who understand".
- **ကြ** marks a plural subject on the verb: မှားကြတယ်.
- **ဒါကြောင့်** means "therefore".
- **V + တုန်း** means "while V".
- **reduplicated adjective + နဲ့** turns the adjective into an adverb: ဇွဲရှိရှိနဲ့, "persistently".
- **V + ကြည့်** means "try V-ing": လုပ်ကြည့်ပါ.
- **V + ပေး** means "do V for someone": ရေးပေးပါ.

## 3. Two opinion adjectives together: general vs specific opinion adjectives

**Rule (English):** Opinion adjectives split into **general** ones and **specific** ones.
- **General** opinion adjectives (good, nice, wonderful, excellent, bad) can describe almost any noun.
- **Specific** opinion adjectives (e.g. tasty) only fit certain nouns.
- When two opinion adjectives come together, the **general** one comes before the **specific** one. Example: *a nice tasty soup*. This rule is inferred from the lesson structure; the frames in this batch show the classification, not the final ordering slide.

Burmese grammar notes (inferred):
- **ဆိုရင်** means "if (it is) / as for".
- **ဘယ်လို … မလဲ** is a wh-question with future/irrealis မ + question particle လဲ.
- **တွေချည်းပဲ** means "only / nothing but (plural)".
- **V + ရင်ကော** means "what about if V?".
- **နှစ်ခုလုံး** means "both (things)"; **နှစ်လုံး** means "two (words)".
- **ဘယ်… မဆို** means "any … at all, whatever": ဘယ်နေရာမဆို, "anywhere".
- **V + လို့ရ** means "can / is allowed".
- **V + ရ** means "must / have to": နေရာရွေးသုံးရ.
- **N + နဲ့သာ** means "only with N".
- **တွဲသုံး** means "use together / collocate".
